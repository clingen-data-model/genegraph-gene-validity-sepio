#+AUTHOR: comparison notes

* Sources compared

- *Phenopackets v2*: ~/Users/tristan/code/phenopacket-schema/src/main/proto/phenopackets/schema/v2~
  (~phenopackets.proto~ plus ~core/{base,biosample,disease,genome,individual,interpretation,
  measurement,medical_action,meta_data,pedigree,phenotypic_feature}.proto~).
- *SEPIO transform*: this codebase, primarily
  ~src/genegraph/transform/gene_validity/sepio_model.clj~ and the SPARQL CONSTRUCT
  templates in ~sepio_model/*.sparql~ that produce the ~cg:Statement~ / ~cg:EvidenceLine~
  graph emitted to the ~gene-validity-sepio~ topic.

* Purpose and orientation

| Axis              | Phenopackets v2                                                                                         | SEPIO Gene-Validity transform                                                                                                |
|-------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| What it describes | A *patient-level record*: an individual (or family/cohort) with phenotypes, samples, variants, actions. | A *knowledge-level assertion*: a curated claim that a gene causes a disease, supported by evidence aggregated across studies. |
| Granularity       | One subject (or one pedigree).                                                                          | One gene/disease/MOI proposition; evidence rolls up many probands, families, papers.                                          |
| Schema technology | Protobuf (proto3); serializes to JSON.                                                                  | RDF (Turtle/JSON-LD) over a ClinGen ~cg:~ vocabulary, generated by SPARQL CONSTRUCT.                                         |
| Schema posture    | Closed message types; field-numbered, evolution-by-version.                                             | Open RDF graph; classes-as-instances; SHACL for shape validation.                                                            |
| Distribution unit | A ~Phenopacket~ / ~Family~ / ~Cohort~ message.                                                          | A versioned ~cg:Statement~ rooted at a ~cg:GeneValidityProposition~, published to Kafka.                                     |

* Where the models clearly *agree*

** Both are ontology-anchored
Both treat HPO/MONDO/SO/etc. CURIEs as the primary semantic backbone. Phenopackets
wraps each in an ~OntologyClass~; the SEPIO graph uses the IRI directly (e.g. the
mode of inheritance HPO term is constructed from a string in
~construct_proposition.sparql~).

** Both link evidence to publications
- Phenopackets: ~Evidence.reference~ ~ExternalReference~ with ~PMID:...~ id.
- SEPIO: every evidence item carries ~dc:source <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/{pmid}>~,
  and ~construct_articles.sparql~ optionally materialises article metadata as a
  ~dc:BibliographicResource~.

** Both can carry an ECO-style evidence type
- Phenopackets: ~Evidence.evidence_code~ (ECO).
- SEPIO: ~cg:specifiedBy <Criteria>~ on each ~cg:EvidenceLine~ — analogous in
  intent (which kind of evidence), but anchored in ClinGen-specific criteria
  (~cg:GeneValidityNullVariantCriteria~, ~cg:GeneValidityPatientCellFunctionalAlterationCriteria~,
  …) instead of the generic ECO hierarchy.

** Both model probands within families
- Phenopackets: ~Family.proband~ + ~relatives~ + ~Pedigree~.
- SEPIO: ~cg:Family cg:member ?proband~ produced by ~construct_proband_score.sparql~
  / ~construct_proband_segregation_evidence.sparql~.

** Both record contributors / timestamps
- Phenopackets: ~MetaData.created~, ~created_by~, ~submitted_by~, ~Update.timestamp~.
- SEPIO: ~cg:Contribution~ nodes built in ~construct_statement.sparql~ and
  ~construct_secondary_contributions.sparql~ — typed by ~cg:activityType~
  (~cg:Evaluated~, ~cg:Submitted~, ~cg:Unpublished~, ~cg:SecondaryContribution~).

* Where the models *diverge*

** Knowledge vs. data
Phenopackets is a *data* record about a subject; the closest thing to a knowledge
claim it carries is an ~Interpretation~/~Diagnosis~ for that subject. SEPIO emits a
*claim*: a ~cg:Statement~ that asserts gene–disease validity at a strength level,
parameterised by a ClinGen SOP. The SEPIO model has no notion of "this is a
particular person's record"; the proband-level data it does carry is *evidence*
for the claim.

** Evidence aggregation tree
Phenopackets has *no* concept of weighted evidence aggregation. ~Evidence~ is a
flat ~{evidence_code, reference}~ struct attached to a ~PhenotypicFeature~.

The SEPIO transform builds a 3–4 level *evidence-line tree*:

#+begin_example
cg:Statement
└── cg:hasEvidenceLines  cg:Overall(Genetic|Experimental)EvidenceCriteria
    └── cg:hasEvidenceLines  category EL (e.g. AD null variants, Functional, Model+Rescue)
        └── cg:hasEvidenceLines  per-criterion EL (e.g. ProbandADNullCriteria)
            └── cg:hasEvidenceItems  evidence item (Proband, Family, Cohort, Finding…)
#+end_example

Each ~cg:EvidenceLine~ carries ~cg:score~ / ~cg:strengthScore~,
~cg:calculatedScore~, ~cg:direction~ (Supports/Disputes/Neutral), and is
~cg:specifiedBy~ a curation criterion. Cross-criteria wiring is generated by
~construct_evidence_connections.sparql~ from ~gdm_sepio_relationships.ttl~. None
of this exists in Phenopackets.

** Classification semantics
- Phenopackets: ~InterpretationStatus~ (REJECTED / CANDIDATE / CONTRIBUTORY /
  CAUSATIVE) per genomic feature, plus ~AcmgPathogenicityClassification~
  (BENIGN…PATHOGENIC) per variant.
- SEPIO: ~cg:classification~ on the ~cg:Statement~ valued from
  {~cg:Definitive~, ~cg:Strong~, ~cg:Moderate~, ~cg:Limited~, ~cg:Disputes~,
  ~cg:Refutes~, ~cg:NoKnownDiseaseRelationship~, …} plus a ~cg:direction~
  derived in ~construct_statement.sparql~. There is *no* per-variant ACMG
  pathogenicity classification — variant strength is encoded as a numeric
  score against a curation criterion.

** Variant representation
| Aspect              | Phenopackets                                                  | SEPIO                                                                                                     |
|---------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Descriptor          | ~VariationDescriptor~ (VRSatile / VRS canonical id)           | ~ga4gh:VariationDescriptor~ with ~ga4gh:CanonicalReference~ → CAID or ClinVar variation id                |
| ACMG class          | ~AcmgPathogenicityClassification~ enum                        | not modelled                                                                                              |
| Therapeutic context | ~TherapeuticActionability~ enum                               | not modelled                                                                                              |
| Allele origin       | (not in VariantInterpretation)                                | ~cg:alleleOrigin~ ∈ {~cg:DeNovoAlleleOrigin~, ~cg:GermlineAlleleOrigin~, ~cg:AlleleOrigin~}              |
| Zygosity            | (not in VariantInterpretation)                                | ~cg:zygosity~ ∈ {Heterozygous, Homozygous, Hemizygous, biallelic in-trans variants…}                      |
| Paternity confirmed | not modelled                                                  | ~cg:paternityMaternityConfirmed~                                                                          |
| Functional impact   | not modelled (lives outside the variant interpretation)       | ~cg:VariantFunctionalImpactEvidence~ attached to the variant evidence line                                |

** Phenotype detail
- Phenopackets ~PhenotypicFeature~ is rich: ~excluded~ (negation), ~severity~,
  ~modifiers~, ~onset~, ~resolution~, ~evidence~.
- SEPIO models phenotypes as a flat list of HPO IRIs on ~cg:Proband~
  (~cg:phenotypes~) plus ~cg:phenotypeFreeText~. There is no per-phenotype
  severity/modifier/onset/excluded structure.

** Time and age
- Phenopackets ~TimeElement~: oneof ~GestationalAge~ | ISO-8601 ~Age~ |
  ~AgeRange~ | ~OntologyClass~ | ~Timestamp~ | ~TimeInterval~.
- SEPIO carries proband age as the unparsed GCI triple ~cg:ageType~ /
  ~cg:ageUnit~ / ~cg:ageValue~ (string-typed), and contribution dates as
  ~cg:date~ strings. No ISO-8601 duration support, no gestational age, no
  intervals.

** Sex and demographics
- Phenopackets distinguishes ~Sex~ (administrative), ~KaryotypicSex~ (XX/XY/XO/…),
  self-identified ~gender~ (OntologyClass), and ~taxonomy~ (NCBITaxon).
- SEPIO has only ~cg:sex~ and ~cg:ethnicity~ on ~cg:Proband~ / ~cg:Family~,
  pass-through from GCI strings. No karyotypic sex; no taxonomy (organism is
  always assumed human at the proband level — model-organism evidence is
  modelled separately as ~cg:StudyResult~).

** Pedigree vs. cosegregation
- Phenopackets ~Pedigree.Person~ encodes paternal/maternal IDs and an
  ~AffectedStatus~ enum — a *structural* pedigree.
- SEPIO ~cg:FamilyCosegregation~ encodes the *quantitative* segregation
  analysis: ~cg:estimatedLodScore~, ~cg:publishedLodScore~,
  ~cg:phenotypePositiveAllelePositive~, ~cg:phenotypeNegativeAlleleNegative~,
  ~cg:sequencingMethod~, ~cg:meetsInclusionCriteria~. The relationships
  themselves are not enumerated person-by-person.

** Disease detail
- Phenopackets ~Disease~ has ~excluded~, ~onset~, ~resolution~, ~disease_stage~,
  ~clinical_tnm_finding~, ~primary_site~, ~laterality~ — plenty of
  oncology-shaped fields.
- SEPIO carries only the disease IRI as ~cg:objectCondition~ on the
  proposition. No staging, no laterality, no per-curation onset.

** Things in Phenopackets that have *no* SEPIO counterpart
- ~Biosample~ (tissue, sample type, tumor staging, processing) — not modelled.
- ~Measurement~ (assays, quantities, reference ranges) — not modelled.
- ~MedicalAction~ (treatments, radiation, regimens) — not modelled.
- ~VitalStatus~ (alive/deceased, cause of death, survival) — not modelled.
- ~File~ (URI to VCF / data files, with sample mapping) — not modelled.
- ~Resource~ list (per-record ontology version registry inside ~MetaData~) — not
  modelled; ontology vocabularies are global to the framework instead.

** Things in SEPIO that have *no* Phenopackets counterpart
- *Curation criteria taxonomy* (~cg:GeneValidityProbandADNullCriteria~,
  ~cg:GeneValidityPatientCellFunctionalAlterationCriteria~, …) and the
  ~gdm_sepio_relationships.ttl~ shape that wires criteria into the evidence-line
  hierarchy.
- *SOP versioning of the criteria themselves* (~cg:GeneValidityCriteria5/6/7/8~
  selected by ~construct_statement.sparql~).
- *Case–control evidence* (~cg:Finding~ with ~cg:caseCohort~/~cg:controlCohort~,
  p-values, confidence intervals). Phenopackets has no notion of cohort-level
  statistical evidence.
- *Functional / functional-alteration / model-system / rescue evidence*
  (~cg:VariantFunctionalImpactEvidence~, ~cg:FunctionalAlteration~,
  ~cg:StudyResult cg:interpretation cg:ProteinAlterationDisruptsOrganismFunction~,
  ~cg:Finding cg:demonstrates cg:PhenotypeRescue~). Phenopackets is silent on
  experimental gene-function evidence.
- *Mode of inheritance as a proposition qualifier* (~cg:qualifierModeOfInheritance~,
  HPO subclasses of ~HP:0000005~).
- *Publish / unpublish lifecycle* with explicit
  ~cg:activityType cg:Unpublished~ contributions and an ~unpublish_date~ on the
  downstream stakeholder event.
- *Semantic versioning of the assertion* (~v{major}.{minor}~ suffix, change-type
  detection — classification / MOI / SOP / evidence / summary text changes —
  produced by ~versioning.clj~ and ~website_events.clj~).
- *Direction of evidence* (~cg:direction~ ∈ {~cg:Supports~, ~cg:Disputes~,
  ~cg:Neutral~}) at every level of the evidence tree.
- *Score arithmetic* (~cg:strengthScore~, ~cg:calculatedScore~, capped proband
  scores from ~add-proband-scores~ in ~sepio_model.clj~, recessive-zygosity
  score doubling in ~construct_variant_score.sparql~).

* Same-thing, different-shape attributes

This section catalogues attributes that *both* schemas carry but encode in
materially different ways — i.e. cases where a translator would need a
per-attribute coercion, not just a property rename.

** Age of the subject

- *Phenopackets* (~base.proto~ ~TimeElement~): a oneof of ~GestationalAge {weeks, days}~,
  ISO-8601 ~Age {iso8601duration}~ (e.g. ~"P40Y10M05D"~), ~AgeRange {start, end}~,
  ~OntologyClass~ (HPO age-of-onset term), ~Timestamp~, or ~TimeInterval~.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_proband_score.sparql~, ~construct_variant_score.sparql~):
  three independent string-typed predicates on ~cg:Proband~ — ~cg:ageType~
  (e.g. onset/encounter), ~cg:ageUnit~ (year/month/…), ~cg:ageValue~ (raw
  numeric string).
- *Coercion*: SEPIO ~{ageValue=40, ageUnit=Year}~ → Phenopackets ~Age "P40Y"~.
  No round-trip for gestational age, intervals, ranges, or ontology-class onset.

** Sex of the subject

- *Phenopackets* (~individual.proto~): three orthogonal fields —
  ~Sex~ enum (~UNKNOWN_SEX~/~FEMALE~/~MALE~/~OTHER_SEX~), ~KaryotypicSex~ enum
  (XX/XY/XO/XXY/XXX/XXYY/XXXY/XXXX/XYY/OTHER), and ~OntologyClass gender~.
- *SEPIO*: a single ~cg:sex~ literal on ~cg:Proband~, pass-through from the GCI
  free-text field. No karyotype, no separate gender identity.
- *Coercion*: lossy in both directions — Phenopackets karyotype/gender cannot
  be expressed in SEPIO; SEPIO's free string must be normalised before mapping
  to a Phenopackets ~Sex~ enum.

** Variant / allele identifier

- *Phenopackets* (~interpretation.proto~ + VRSatile): ~VariationDescriptor~
  embedding a VRS object (canonical, hashed allele) plus
  ~AcmgPathogenicityClassification~ and ~TherapeuticActionability~ enums.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_alleles.sparql~): ~ga4gh:VariationDescriptor~ with
  ~ga4gh:CanonicalReference~ resolved to *either* a ClinGen Allele Registry
  CAID (~http://reg.genome.network/allele/...~) *or* a ClinVar variation IRI
  (~https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/...~), and a single
  ~skos:prefLabel~ chosen from the first non-empty of canonicalTranscriptTitle,
  maneTranscriptTitle, preferredTitle, clinvarVariantTitle, GRCh38 HGVS, or
  GRCh37 HGVS.
- *Coercion*: both share VRSatile shape, but SEPIO uses CAID/ClinVar as the
  identity anchor while Phenopackets uses the VRS canonical id. ACMG class and
  therapeutic actionability have no SEPIO carrier.

** Phenotype observation

- *Phenopackets* (~phenotypic_feature.proto~): structured ~PhenotypicFeature~
  with ~type~ (OntologyClass), ~excluded~, ~severity~, ~modifiers[]~, ~onset~
  (TimeElement), ~resolution~ (TimeElement), ~evidence[]~.
- *SEPIO*: a flat list on ~cg:Proband~ — ~cg:phenotypes~ (HPO IRIs, no
  modifier slot) plus ~cg:phenotypeFreeText~ for unstructured terms.
- *Coercion*: each SEPIO HPO IRI maps to ~PhenotypicFeature.type~ with
  everything else default; SEPIO loses excluded/severity/modifier/onset on
  ingest.

** Disease / condition

- *Phenopackets* (~disease.proto~): ~Disease~ with ~term~, ~excluded~, ~onset~,
  ~resolution~, ~disease_stage[]~, ~clinical_tnm_finding[]~, ~primary_site~,
  ~laterality~.
- *SEPIO*: a single ~cg:objectCondition~ IRI on the ~cg:GeneValidityProposition~.
  No staging/onset/laterality/negation — the proposition is asserted *about*
  the disease, not *describing* it for a patient.
- *Coercion*: trivial in the SEPIO→Phenopackets direction (term only); the
  reverse drops everything but ~term~.

** Pedigree / kinship

- *Phenopackets* (~pedigree.proto~): ~Pedigree.Person~ records with
  ~family_id~, ~individual_id~, ~paternal_id~, ~maternal_id~, ~Sex~,
  ~AffectedStatus~ (MISSING/UNAFFECTED/AFFECTED). Structural — describes
  who is whose parent.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_proband_segregation_evidence.sparql~,
  ~construct_family_segregation_evidence.sparql~): ~cg:Family~ with
  ~cg:member~ links to probands and a ~cg:FamilyCosegregation~ blob carrying
  ~cg:phenotypePositiveAllelePositive~ / ~cg:phenotypeNegativeAlleleNegative~
  *counts* and LOD scores. Quantitative — describes how many relatives
  segregate, not who they are.
- *Coercion*: bidirectionally lossy. A Phenopackets pedigree could in principle
  be summarised into SEPIO co-segregation counts, but the per-person
  identities and parent links would not survive a round-trip.

** Citation / publication

- *Phenopackets* (~base.proto~): ~ExternalReference {id, reference, description}~
  embedded inside ~Evidence~ alongside an ECO ~evidence_code~.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_articles.sparql~): a ~dc:BibliographicResource~ keyed by
  the resolved PubMed IRI ~https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/{pmid}~, with
  ~dc:title~, ~dc:creator~ (first author only), ~dc:date~ (year-only),
  ~dc:abstract~. Linked from each evidence item by ~dc:source~.
- *Coercion*: SEPIO ~dc:source~ IRI ↔ Phenopackets ~ExternalReference.reference~;
  ~dc:title~ ↔ ~ExternalReference.description~. Phenopackets bundles its
  ECO type into the same struct, whereas SEPIO carries it separately on the
  surrounding ~cg:EvidenceLine~ via ~cg:specifiedBy~.

** Authoring contribution

- *Phenopackets* (~meta_data.proto~): ~MetaData~ string fields ~created_by~ /
  ~submitted_by~ + a ~Timestamp created~ + an ~Update~ history list (timestamp,
  updated_by, comment).
- *SEPIO* (~construct_statement.sparql~,
  ~construct_secondary_contributions.sparql~): one or more ~cg:Contribution~
  nodes with ~cg:contributor~ (affiliation IRI under
  ~https://genegraph.clinicalgenome.org/agent/~), ~cg:date~ literal, and
  ~cg:activityType~ from {~cg:Evaluated~, ~cg:Submitted~, ~cg:Unpublished~,
  ~cg:SecondaryContribution~}.
- *Coercion*: SEPIO Evaluated→~MetaData.created~/~created_by~,
  Submitted→~MetaData.updates~ entry. Phenopackets has no slot for the
  Unpublished activity type or for distinguishing primary vs. secondary
  contributors as classes of provenance.

** Mode of inheritance

- *Phenopackets*: not first-class. The closest carrier would be a
  ~PhenotypicFeature~ with an HPO MOI term, or ~Family.consanguinous_parents~
  for the special case of consanguinity.
- *SEPIO*: ~cg:qualifierModeOfInheritance~ on the proposition, valued as an
  HPO mode-of-inheritance IRI built in ~construct_proposition.sparql~ from
  the GCI ~modeInheritance~ string.
- *Coercion*: SEPIO MOI maps cleanly into a Phenopackets HPO ontology class but
  loses its qualifier-of-claim semantics.

** Zygosity and allele origin

- *Phenopackets*: not modelled at the ~VariantInterpretation~ level — would
  need a VRSatile extension or convention.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_variant_score.sparql~): ~cg:zygosity~ ∈ {Heterozygous,
  Homozygous, Hemizygous, biallelic in-trans variants…} and ~cg:alleleOrigin~
  ∈ {~cg:DeNovoAlleleOrigin~, ~cg:GermlineAlleleOrigin~, ~cg:AlleleOrigin~}.
- *Coercion*: one-way only — SEPIO → Phenopackets requires custom extension
  fields; Phenopackets → SEPIO has no source.

** Sequencing / detection method

- *Phenopackets*: implicit — any technique would be carried as the
  ~Procedure.code~ of a ~Measurement~ or ~Biosample~.
- *SEPIO*: four flat predicates on ~cg:Proband~ —
  ~cg:firstTestingMethod~, ~cg:secondTestingMethod~, ~cg:detectionMethod~,
  ~cg:sequencingMethod~ (the last on ~cg:FamilyCosegregation~) — pulled out
  of the GCI ~method.genotypingMethods~ list and ~specificMutationsGenotypedMethod~
  via ~rdf:rest~/~rdf:first~ list traversal.
- *Coercion*: SEPIO method strings → ontology terms required before they fit
  Phenopackets' ~Procedure.code~ shape.

** Statistical evidence (case–control)

- *Phenopackets*: not modelled. The nearest analogue is a ~Measurement~ with
  a ~Quantity~ and ~ReferenceRange~, but case/control cohort comparison has no
  native shape.
- *SEPIO* (~construct_case_control_evidence.sparql~): ~cg:Finding~ with
  ~cg:caseCohort~/~cg:controlCohort~ (each a ~cg:Cohort~ with
  ~cg:allGenotypedSequenced~, ~cg:numWithVariant~, ~cg:alleleFrequency~,
  ~cg:detectionMethod~), plus ~cg:pValue~, ~cg:statisticalSignificanceType~,
  ~cg:statisticalSignificanceValueType~, ~cg:statisticalSignificanceValue~,
  ~cg:lowerConfidenceLimit~, ~cg:upperConfidenceLimit~.
- *Coercion*: not feasible without a Phenopackets extension.

** Family / individual identifiers

- *Phenopackets*: free string ~id~ scoped to the record (~Family.id~,
  ~Individual.id~, ~Pedigree.Person.individual_id~).
- *SEPIO*: full IRIs in the ~https://genegraph.clinicalgenome.org/r/~ namespace,
  derived from the GCI record id and suffixed (e.g.
  ~..._proband_score_evidence_line~, ~..._family_segregation~, ~..._cc_el~).
- *Coercion*: SEPIO IRI tail → Phenopackets ~id~; reverse needs a base IRI
  convention.

** Cohort / group

- *Phenopackets* (~phenopackets.proto~): ~Cohort~ — a top-level message
  bundling ~Phenopacket~ members for a group study.
- *SEPIO*: ~cg:Cohort~ — a *case or control arm* inside a single case-control
  study, with aggregate counts rather than enumerated members.
- *Coercion*: false friend. The names match but the level of analysis does not
  — a Phenopackets ~Cohort~ would map to a *set* of SEPIO probands, not to a
  SEPIO ~cg:Cohort~.

* Bottom line

Phenopackets and the SEPIO Gene-Validity model occupy *adjacent but disjoint*
layers of the same problem space. Phenopackets standardises the *clinical and
genomic record of a subject* — what was observed, what was sampled, what
treatment was given, what diagnosis was made. The SEPIO transform standardises
the *expert curation of gene–disease validity* — what evidence supports the
claim that variation in this gene causes this disease under this mode of
inheritance, scored against a ClinGen SOP.

They overlap on the substrate (HPO phenotypes, PMID citations, variant
descriptors, family/proband structure), and a single curation could in
principle ingest Phenopackets as one source of its proband evidence. They
diverge on everything that is unique to either side: Phenopackets has the
clinical/sample/measurement detail that SEPIO discards; SEPIO has the
evidence-aggregation tree, scoring, criteria taxonomy, MOI qualifier, lifecycle
and versioning that Phenopackets does not attempt.
